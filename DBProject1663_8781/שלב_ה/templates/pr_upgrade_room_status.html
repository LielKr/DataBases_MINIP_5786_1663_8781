pr_upgrade_room_status(min_rating) — Scans all guest feedback and moves rooms that received a rating at or below the specified threshold to MAINTENANCE status. Also logs the reason in the ROOM_MAINTENANCE_LOG table.

Run Procedure
Rooms with feedback at or below this rating will be moved to MAINTENANCE
How It Works
  1. Scans GUEST_FEEDBACK for ratings ≤ threshold
  2. Finds the rooms assigned to those stays
  3. Updates room status to MAINTENANCE
  4. Logs the action in ROOM_MAINTENANCE_LOG
